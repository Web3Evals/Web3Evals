01

Why Web3Evals

Capability needs a measurement layer.

Security-flavored output is not the same as security capability.

Model launches move quickly. Product demonstrations are selective. Public benchmarks can become familiar to the systems they measure. Web3Evals is being built to provide a clearer, independently produced signal for Web3 security work.

A

Grounded work

Tasks are designed around work that matters to security practitioners, not trivia or security-themed prose.

B

Controlled runs

Systems are compared under documented conditions so differences are easier to interpret and reproduce.

C

Useful evidence

Results are intended to expose what a system did, not collapse the entire evaluation into a marketing number.

04

Questions

The short version.

What is Web3Evals?

An independent evaluation project focused on how LLMs and agentic systems perform on applied Web3 security tasks.

When will SCAR v1 be released?

There is no announced date yet. The evaluation and its release materials will ship when the methodology and results are ready for scrutiny.

Will the methodology be public?

Each public release will document the conditions and limitations needed to interpret and reproduce its published results.

Can a model or security-tool team participate?

Participation details will be shared with the release. Teams can register interest now through the contact address below.

Is leaderboard placement for sale?

No. Any material relationship relevant to an evaluation will be disclosed, and commercial placement will not determine results.
